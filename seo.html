FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Have Questions?

Here are some common questions about the Search Engine Optimization course.

Do I need web development experience?

No. The course is designed for beginners. Basic computer and internet knowledge is helpful but previous SEO experience is not required.

What topics are covered in SEO?

The course covers SEO fundamentals, keyword research, on-page SEO, technical SEO, off-page concepts, content optimization and basic SEO analysis.

Is SEO only about using keywords?

No. Keywords are one part of SEO. The course introduces students to broader areas including website structure, content, technical factors and off-page concepts.

Can I use SEO skills for freelance work?

SEO knowledge can be used for freelance services such as keyword research, website audits, content optimization and basic SEO support.

Will I receive a certificate?

The course catalogue currently presents certificate completion as a course benefit. Final certificate requirements can be customized by the academy.
