Course Overview

Design Spaces With Creativity & Purpose

The Interior Designing course introduces students to the fundamentals of planning and designing residential and commercial spaces.

You will learn how to understand a client's requirements, organize space efficiently, select suitable colors and materials, plan furniture layouts, and create attractive interior concepts.

The course combines design theory with practical exercises so students can gradually develop a professional design portfolio.

What You'll Learn

Build Practical Interior Design Skills

Develop the essential skills required to plan, visualize, and present interior spaces professionally.

Space Planning

Learn how to organize rooms, furniture, circulation, and functional zones effectively.

Color Theory

Understand color combinations, mood, balance, contrast, and visual harmony.

Furniture Planning

Explore furniture placement, proportions, functionality, and room composition.

Lighting Design

Learn how lighting affects atmosphere, functionality, and visual appearance.

Materials & Textures

Discover flooring, walls, fabrics, wood, finishes, textures, and decorative materials.

Design Presentation

Learn how to present concepts, layouts, mood boards, and design ideas clearly.

Frequently Asked Questions

Interior Designing Course FAQ

Is this course suitable for beginners?

Yes. The course starts with basic interior design concepts and gradually moves toward practical projects.

Do I need previous design experience?

No previous professional interior design experience is required for this beginner-level course.

What type of spaces will I learn to design?

You will explore residential spaces such as living rooms, bedrooms, kitchens, and other functional areas.

Will I create practical projects?

Yes. Practical exercises and a final interior concept project will help you develop work for your portfolio.

Can I work as a freelancer after the course?

The skills can be used to develop a portfolio and explore freelance or entry-level design opportunities.

Will I receive a certificate?

Yes. Students who successfully complete the course can receive a course completion certificate.
